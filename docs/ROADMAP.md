# What to add next

| Priority | Addition | Decision improved | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prospective thesis and forecast register | Distinguish process quality from hindsight | Timestamped note before the event; probability, horizon, resolution rule and later Brier score; keep all forecasts |
| 2 | Full operating models for CME, Novo and Rheinmetall | Separate business growth from valuation | Source-linked revenue drivers, margins, cash conversion, reinvestment and share count; reconciled statements; bear/base/bull cases |
| 3 | Reference execution and corporate-action ledger | Make forward P&L auditable | Separate decision, order, fill, ex-date entitlement, payment and mark timestamps; documented transaction costs |
| 4 | Fund look-through and factor exposures | Expose hidden concentration | Dated underlying holdings and currency/sector/country aggregation; preserve vendor vintage |
| 5 | Executable FX carry pilot | Test financing rather than a policy-rate story | Bid/ask forward points, actual paper financing convention, margin stress and EUR reconciliation |
| 6 | Risk-matched benchmarks and tracking error | Assess allocation choices fairly | Same base currency, costs, risk budget and cash convention; compare before looking for a winning specification |
| 7 | Monthly post-mortem and rejected ideas | Demonstrate judgement and learning | What changed, what was wrong, why no trade was made, and how the next decision improves |

These are development priorities, not claims that the unfinished capabilities already exist. Avoid adding many complex models before owning a few strong investment cases. A current, reconciled operating model for a company you understand is more useful than an unexplained machine-learning prediction.
