# Research process

The portfolio has two distinct records: a reconstructed historical study and a forward paper process. Neither is real-money investing. A later model can evaluate an old decision without having been available to the investor at that time.

For each new idea, create a dated note using `DECISION_TEMPLATE.md`. Separate the economic hypothesis, the priced-in expectation, the vehicle and the portfolio risk. Define the observation that would change your mind before looking at the next outcome. Compare a trade with doing nothing, and compare both at similar risk where possible.

The model sequence is: reconcile the book; verify source availability and publication dates; articulate assumptions; run the applicable model; inspect adverse scenarios and parameter sensitivity; set position size; record a prospective paper instruction; then reconcile actual reference fills. Unfilled instructions never create realised P&L.

The frozen research compares three rules and four cost assumptions. All are reported; no winner is silently selected as the future strategy. The chosen universe, events and thresholds were specified with hindsight. Running decisions in date order is necessary, but does not make this a held-out test.

Research is AI-assisted. AI helped implement, document and verify the package. Ownership means understanding the assumptions, reviewing the sources, reproducing the numbers and making independent decisions. Do not describe the package as wholly independently authored or as evidence of two years of live management.

The first prospective improvement should be a publicly dated thesis with a probability forecast and a fixed review date. Keep losing ideas, rejected trades and decisions to hold. A transparent record of learning is more credible than a retrospectively polished sequence of correct calls.
